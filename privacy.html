🐔 Chicken Butt Merge: Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 10, 2026

In short: Chicken Butt Merge does not ask for, collect or store any personal information. There are no accounts, no chat and no sign-in. Game progress is saved only on your device. The game shows optional video ads from Google AdMob, set up for children: no personalized ads and family-friendly content only.

Who we are

Chicken Butt Merge ("the game") is made by EVOKIN. If you have any questions about this policy, email us at [CONTACT EMAIL].

Information we do not collect

  • We do not ask for names, email addresses, phone numbers, photos or locations.
  • There are no player accounts, no messaging and no way to talk to other players.
  • The game does not use the microphone, camera, contacts or location.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (coins, eggs, friends found, outfits and sound settings) is saved only on your own device so you can continue playing later. It is never sent to us. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Voice

Some lines are read aloud by your device's built-in text-to-speech. This happens on your device; we do not receive any audio or text from it.

Advertising

The game can show optional reward videos (for example, "watch a video to get more eggs") and, now and then, a short ad break at a natural pause in play (never in the middle of play, and at most a few per session). Ads come from Google AdMob. Reward videos are always the player's choice, and ad breaks can be closed. We have set AdMob up for a children's audience:

  • Ad requests are marked as directed to children and under the age of consent.
  • Ads are not personalized, and the content rating is limited to "General" (suitable for families).
  • The game does not collect the device's advertising ID.

To deliver ads and prevent fraud, Google may process limited technical information such as the device type, operating system and IP address, as described in how Google uses information in ads and the Google Privacy Policy.

Purchases

If the game offers in-app purchases, they are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see or store payment details.

Children's privacy

The game is made for children and families. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. We follow the Google Play Families Policy, and the advertising is set up to comply with children's privacy laws such as COPPA (USA) and GDPR-K (EU). If you are a parent or guardian and believe information about your child has been collected, please contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above.
